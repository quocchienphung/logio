Usage-based billing launch

Emma Collins · Target Nov 18, 2026

On November 18 every Team workspace moves to usage-based billing: seats plus metered agent runs, invoiced monthly through Stripe.

Goals

Customers should see what they will pay before the invoice arrives. Surprise bills drove 31% of billing tickets in Q3, so a live usage meter and spend alerts ship on day one.

Rollout

Week one goes to 5% of workspaces behind the usage_billing flag. We widen to 50% once refund requests stay under 0.4% for seven days, then ship to everyone on December 2.
