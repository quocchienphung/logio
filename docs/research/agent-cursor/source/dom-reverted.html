Usage-based billing launch

Emma Collins · Target Nov 18, 2026

On November 18 every Team workspace moves to usage-based billing: seats plus metered agent runs, invoiced monthly through Stripe.

Goals

So basically the main thing we are trying to do here is make it so customers can actually see what they are going to pay before the invoice shows up, because a lot of people were surprised by their bills last quarter and that turned into a lot of support tickets.

Week one goes to 5% of workpsaces behind the usage_billing flag. We widen to 50% once refund requests stay under 0.4% for seven days, then ship to everyone on December 2.
