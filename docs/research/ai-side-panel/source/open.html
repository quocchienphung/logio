Team workspaces

Product spec · Maya Chen · Edited Oct 18

Team workspaces are a new feature that will make it possible for people who work together to share their projects, files and billing in one single place, so that they no longer need to forward invoices around or invite each other to every project one at a time.

Goals

Cut the time it takes to invite a team from six minutes to under one. Move 30% of paid solo accounts to a team plan by the end of March 2027.

Pricing

Per seat, billed monthly. Admins add or remove seats at any time and changes are prorated on the next invoice.
