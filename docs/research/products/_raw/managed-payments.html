The opportunity

Selling globally takes more than accepting payments. The infrastructure required to enter, localize, and stay compliant in each market can be a barrier to growth.

Launching in a new market should be a growth opportunity, but it can become a costly lift. Handling tax registrations and filings; localizing checkout; and standing up processes for fraud, disputes, and customer support can slow expansion and make growth more expensive.

Stripe Managed Payments handles tax compliance, checkout, fraud, disputes, and customer support for digital goods businesses. Built on Stripe’s payments infrastructure, it gives you the reliability, performance, and flexibility to scale with less operational overhead.

Frequently asked questions

Learn more about Managed Payments.

What businesses should use Managed Payments?

Managed Payments is designed for businesses selling digital products and software subscriptions. It’s ideal for:

  • ​

    SaaS subscriptions, digital content, and virtual goods

  • ​

    Teams expanding into new countries or selling globally from day one

  • ​

    App developers moving purchases from app stores to the web

  • ​

    Businesses that want to shift tax liability, fraud, disputes, and customer support to Stripe

What is the pricing for Managed Payments?

Managed Payments adds 3.5% per successful transaction to your standard Stripe processing fees. This fee includes global indirect tax (sales, VAT, GST), fraud prevention, dispute responses, and transaction‑level support through Link. Optional products like Billing and local payment methods are separate. There are no setup fees or monthly minimums—pay only for what you use.

How does Managed Payments work with other Stripe products?

Managed Payments works natively with the Checkout page, payment links, and Billing for one-time and subscription purchases. You can use Stripe Sigma for advanced analytics.

For existing Stripe users, Managed Payments integrates seamlessly with your existing Stripe setup. The key difference is that Stripe (via Link) becomes the merchant of record for Managed Payments transactions, handling global indirect tax (sales, VAT, GST), fraud prevention, dispute responses, and transaction‑level support through Link

How does Stripe Tax compare to Stripe Managed Payments?

With Tax, businesses remain the merchant of record and keep tax liability. Tax automates tax calculation and helps with registration and filing, and your business is responsible for remittance and audits.

With Managed Payments, Stripe (via Link) becomes the merchant of record and is responsible for global indirect tax (sales, VAT, GST) on these transactions in supported jurisdictions—including calculation, collection, filing, and remittance. We also handle fraud protection, dispute responses, and transaction-level customer support.

Can I use Managed Payments selectively for certain markets or products?

Yes, you can enable Managed Payments at the transaction level, choosing exactly when and where to use it. Many businesses keep direct processing in their home market and use Managed Payments for international expansion, or apply it only to specific product lines. This hybrid approach lets you optimize for performance and cost without rebuilding your entire payment system. You can start with a test market, measure the results, and expand based on what works best for your business.

What is Link, and how does it work with Managed Payments?

Link is Stripe’s consumer platform that powers the customer experience for Managed Payments transactions. Customers interact with Link at checkout, on receipts and statements, and after purchase. In their Link account, customers can manage orders and subscriptions, update payment methods, and access support.
