The opportunity

AI has made software value dynamic—but many billing systems haven’t kept up. Metronome lets you charge for the value you deliver, the moment you deliver it.

Customers increasingly expect to pay only for the value they receive. That means product teams need to regularly test and iterate pricing, while finance teams need to track usage, account for revenue accurately, and maintain clear, continuous reporting on all of it.

With Metronome, you can launch new pricing models in days; test and adjust on the fly; and meter, bill, and track revenue in one place—with clear visibility into usage and spend for you and your customers. The result: billing that keeps pace with your product.

Frequently asked questions

Learn more about Metronome.

What types of businesses use Metronome for usage-based billing?

Leading AI and SaaS companies use Metronome to meter and charge customers based on consumption metrics like API calls, tokens, compute hours, outcomes, and more. From AI startups launching their first pricing model to enterprise SaaS businesses navigating complex billing operations, Stripe is a good fit for any business looking for a flexible, scalable solution to monetize AI products and usage-based services.

What pricing models does Metronome support?

Metronome supports a wide range of pricing models: usage-based billing, credit-based pricing, enterprise contracts, and multidimensional and hybrid models. You can combine different models to serve different customer segments and lifecycle stages, supporting both product-led and sales-led growth strategies.

What is Metronome and how does it relate to Stripe?

Metronome is a Stripe product purpose-built for the most sophisticated usage-based billing scenarios. It provides capabilities like multidimensional pricing, centralized rate cards, enterprise contract management, cloud marketplace integrations, and real-time revenue reporting at the account, contract, and product level. Metronome is available as an add-on to Stripe Billing. See pricing for more details.

Can I support both self-serve and sales-led billing on Metronome?

Yes. Metronome supports both motions with a shared billing and pricing foundation, with integration paths for Stripe Checkout, Stripe Tax, and Stripe Payments. Metronome also supports enterprise contracts and cloud marketplace transactions.

How does Metronome handle high-volume usage-based billing?

Metronome handles billions of events per day, 100K+ events per second with low latency, and manages high-cardinality use cases across thousands of SKUs and seats. The fastest-growing businesses rely on Metronome where billing accuracy and reliability are key.
