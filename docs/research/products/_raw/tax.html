Tax compliance made simple

Don’t let taxes hold you back

Businesses are required to collect taxes in more than 170 countries and in most US states. Staying compliant can be challenging, especially as your business grows. Tax rules and rates change constantly, and they vary based on what and where you sell. If you ignore these complexities, you risk paying penalties and interest on top of uncollected taxes.

We built Stripe Tax to simplify tax compliance, so you can focus on scaling your business.

How it works

End-to-end tax compliance solution

1

Integrate in minutes

Add a single line of code to your existing integration, or enable tax collection directly in the Dashboard.

2

Know your obligations

See when and where you have tax obligations. Stripe Tax monitors your transactions and helps you register globally—and submit in new locations in under a minute.

3

Collect taxes automatically

Stripe Tax calculates and collects the right amount of tax based on your customer's location, and it validates VAT IDs when needed.

4

File with confidence

Automate global filings through Stripe or our pre-integrated filing partners. You can also use our detailed reports to file on your own.

Step 1

Get started in minutes

Stripe Tax is built to fit seamlessly into your existing payments and compliance processes, so you can integrate the way you want to.

Add tax without code

Quickly start collecting taxes on Stripe transactions with a single click in the Stripe Dashboard.

Add tax with code

Add tax collection to your custom checkout integration using the Stripe Tax API.

curl https://api.stripe.com/v1/checkout/sessions \ -u sk_test_••••••••••••••••••••••••: \ -d success_url="https://example.com/success" \ -d cancel_url="https://example.com/cancel" \ -d "payment_method_types[0]"=card \ -d "line_items[0][price]"="<PRICE_ID>" \ -d "line_items[0][quantity]"=2 \ -d "automatic_tax[enabled]"=true \ -d mode=payment

  

    ~
Create a Price and Product with the API or through the Dashboard before making this request.

Automate tax with any payment processor

Calculate and report tax on all payments—even ones that are processed outside of Stripe. With the Stripe Tax API, you get the flexibility to use multiple payment processors without added tax complexity.

Step 2

Know your tax obligations and register quickly

Understand where you need to collect taxes, and activate tax collection in a new market in minutes. Let Stripe manage your global tax registrations, saving you time with prefilled application details and reducing errors.

Register today 

See how your Stripe transactions compare to tax filing thresholds, get email and Dashboard alerts when you need to register, and easily start collecting taxes. Let Stripe register for you.

Step 3

Automatically collect taxes

Always calculate and collect the correct amount of tax, no matter what or where you sell. Stripe Tax supports tax collection on hundreds of products and services and keeps tax rules and rates up to date—so you don’t have to.

Tax localization

Reduce checkout friction by showing taxes in the most familiar way to your customers, based on the available location information such as currency or zip code.

Tax ID validation

Stripe Tax helps you collect the tax identification number from B2B customers. You can automatically validate VAT IDs and ABNs for European and Australian customers, and apply a reverse charge or zero VAT rate when necessary.

Step 4

Global filing made easy

Keeping track of filing deadlines and reconciling transactions is time-consuming and can easily lead to mistakes. File US sales tax directly from the Dashboard, powered by TaxJar. Your returns are automatically prepared and filed every period. For global filings, automate across more than 90 countries through Stripe and our filing partners. You can also access location-specific reports to file on your own, with your accountant, or with a preferred partner.
