How we made sync 4x faster for the largest workspaces

Linear made sync about 4x faster for its largest workspaces by replacing the full bootstrap snapshot with partial bootstrapping. The client now loads only what the first screen needs, about 2 percent of the workspace, and streams the rest in the background. Median time to interactive fell from 11.2 to 2.6 seconds, and first-load memory dropped 58 percent.
